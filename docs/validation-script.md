# VakilAI — Customer Validation Script
**Use this for the 20 conversations this week**

---

## Who to Talk To (Priority Order)

1. **Founders / Co-founders** of tech startups (Series A or pre-Series A)
2. **CFOs / Finance Heads** of companies with 50-500 employees
3. **Legal / Compliance Officers** at any Indian tech company
4. **CA / Lawyers** who advise startups (potential referral partners)
5. **CTOs** at companies processing user data (DPDP Act directly affects them)

**Your opening message (WhatsApp / LinkedIn DM):**
> "Hey [Name], I'm building an AI compliance tool for Indian startups — specifically around DPDP Act. Would love 15 minutes to understand how you're handling it. Not selling anything, genuinely researching. When works?"

---

## The Script

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for your time. I'm Alok — I'm building a product around compliance automation for Indian businesses, specifically the DPDP Act. Before I build anything, I want to understand the real pain from people dealing with it. Can I ask you a few questions?"

---

### Section 1: Discovery — Understand Their World (5 min)

1. **"Tell me about your current compliance setup — who owns it in your company?"**
   *(Listen for: is it the founder? A lawyer? A CA? Nobody?)*

2. **"How did you first hear about the DPDP Act, and what's your current understanding of what it requires?"**
   *(Listen for: level of awareness, anxiety, confusion, denial)*

3. **"What have you actually done so far to prepare for DPDP compliance?"**
   *(Listen for: nothing / hired a consultant / using a template / don't know where to start)*

4. **"Do you have a privacy policy? When was it last reviewed?"**
   *(Listen for: yes but 3 years old / copy-paste from internet / proper one)*

5. **"Who do you call when you have a legal or compliance question?"**
   *(Listen for: lawyer on retainer / CA / Google it / WhatsApp a friend)*

---

### Section 2: The Pain (5 min)

6. **"What's the most annoying part of dealing with compliance right now?"**
   *(Let them vent. Don't interrupt. This is gold.)*

7. **"Have you ever been caught off guard by a regulation change or a compliance deadline?"**
   *(Listen for: GST return panic, MCA filing missed, labour law surprise)*

8. **"What happened? What did it cost you — time, money, stress?"**
   *(Quantify the pain)*

9. **"If you had to estimate — how much does your company spend on legal and compliance per year? CA fees, lawyer retainers, everything?"**
   *(This is key. Don't suggest numbers — let them say it first)*

10. **"How long did it take the last time you needed a compliance-related document — like a privacy policy, NDA, or board resolution?"**
    *(Listen for: 2 weeks / ₹30,000 to lawyer / still waiting)*

---

### Section 3: The DPDP Specific Pain (3 min)

11. **"Do you know what a Data Fiduciary is under the DPDP Act?"**
    *(Tests DPDP awareness — most won't know)*

12. **"Are you aware that your company can face penalties up to ₹250 crore under the DPDP Act?"**
    *(Watch the reaction — this usually creates urgency)*

13. **"Have you done a DPDP readiness assessment — formally mapped what personal data you collect and whether your practices comply?"**
    *(Almost nobody has. Follow with:)*

14. **"What's stopping you from doing it?"**
    *(Listen for: don't know where to start / too expensive / not urgent enough / don't have a lawyer)*

---

### Section 4: The Solution Probe (3 min)

*Now introduce the concept — without pitching:*

"So here's what I'm building — tell me honestly what you think."

15. **"Imagine a tool where you answer 30 questions about your business, and in 24 hours it gives you a detailed DPDP compliance report — exactly what you're missing, what's risky, and a prioritized fix list. Think of it like a credit score but for compliance. Would that be useful to you?"**
    *(Listen for: yes but / tell me more / sounds good / we already have that)*

16. **"The report would be equivalent to what a compliance lawyer gives you after a 2-month audit — but delivered in 24 hours. If it cost ₹15,000 as a one-time fee, would you use it?"**
    *(THIS IS THE KEY QUESTION. Watch closely. If they hesitate, ask:)*
    - "What would make it worth ₹15,000 to you?"
    - "What would you normally pay for this?"
    - "If it was ₹5,000, would that change things?"

17. **"If there was also a subscription — say ₹5,000/month — where the tool monitors your compliance continuously, alerts you to regulation changes, and lets you ask unlimited legal questions, is that something your company would budget for?"**

---

### Section 5: Close (2 min)

18. **"This is genuinely useful. Thank you. Can I share an early version with you in 4-6 weeks and get your feedback?"**

19. **"Is there anyone else you think I should talk to — a founder friend, your CA, or a legal person?"**
    *(Ask for 2 referrals every time)*

20. **"Would you be willing to be an early beta user? First 3 months free in exchange for feedback and a testimonial if you love it."**

## Objections & Responses

**"We already have a lawyer who handles this."**
→ "Great — how much do you pay them per year? And how long does it take when you need a document drafted?" *(Usually ₹5-15L/year and 2-3 weeks)*. "What if AI handled 80% of that, and your lawyer just reviewed the final output?"

**"Is AI reliable for legal matters?"**
→ "Good question. We're not replacing your lawyer — we're giving you a first draft and a compliance checklist that your lawyer then validates in 1 hour instead of 10. Think of it like having a brilliant paralegal available 24/7."

**"The DPDP Act rules aren't fully notified yet."**
→ "The core obligations are already in force. Companies processing data of 100,000+ users face the strictest requirements. And enforcement is only going to increase. Every month you wait is a month of risk."

**"We're too small to worry about this."**
→ "Under DPDP, there's no exemption based on company size for most provisions. Even a 10-person startup with 1,000 users has obligations. The fines scale with the violation, not your size."

**"Can't I just use ChatGPT for this?"**
→ "ChatGPT doesn't know Indian law specifically, doesn't cite actual sections, doesn't integrate with your specific company data, and doesn't monitor for regulatory changes. It'll give you generic advice. We give you a compliance report specific to your business."
